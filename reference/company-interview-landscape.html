Three facts to remember before any 2026 loop

  1. Assume AI is banned by default. ~64% of companies still ban AI-assistant use in interviews; treat any exception as something you were explicitly told, not something you infer.
  2. Getting flagged isn't an automatic fail. In the largest dataset found (19,368 interviews), 38.5% were flagged for AI use — yet 61% of flagged candidates still passed. The deciding factor was whether they could explain and debug their own solution live.
  3. The real trained skill is narration under scrutiny. Every anti-cheat strategy converges on the same test: explain your own code, unaided, out loud, while someone watches. Practice that explicitly — it's a distinct skill from solving the problem.
